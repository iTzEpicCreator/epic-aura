Watching on more than one platform?

Link your accounts so all your Epic Aura stacks in one wallet.

  1. On the platform you're on now, type !link youtube YourYouTubeName (swap in twitch or kick and your name there).
  2. Within 10 minutes, go to that other platform and type !link confirm.
  3. Done. Your balances are combined and you earn into the same wallet from both.

Only the account you named can confirm, so nobody can link into your wallet. Changed your mind? !unlink splits that account back out.

Amounts can be a number, all, half or shorthand like 2k.

!fight <legend> <1v1|1v2|1v3> <amount>

Pick a legend, pick your fight. Leave the legend out for a random one, leave the size out for a 1v1. Nicknames work too: pathy, gibby, valk, bh, maggie, newy, cata.

Battle royale

!ring <buy-in> [legend]

Start a lobby with a buy-in, then everyone else types !ring to drop in at the same price. You get 90 seconds to join and need at least 3 players.

Every 12 seconds the ring closes and knocks people out until it's a final 1v1. The champion takes 80% of the pot and second place gets 20%, after a small house cut.

Buy-in 50 to 10,000. One game every 5 minutes.

Duel

!duel <name> <amount>

Challenge someone. They have 60 seconds to type !accept or !deny. It's 50/50 and the winner takes the bet. Works across platforms if your accounts are linked.

Heist

!heist <amount>

The first person starts it, everyone else has 60 seconds to join with their own stake. The bigger the crew, the better the odds. Everyone who makes it out gets 1.8x their stake back.

Min 50. One heist every 5 minutes.

Gamble

!gamble <amount>

Roll 1 to 100. 56 or higher doubles your bet. Min 10, max 10,000, 30 second cooldown.

    Questions

    Is Epic Aura the same as Twitch channel points?

    No. Channel points are Twitch only and are used for the redeems in the Twitch menu. Epic Aura works on Twitch, YouTube and Kick and is used with chat commands.

    Why isn't my balance going up?

    Aura is only paid while the stream is live, and the first few minutes after you arrive don't count. On YouTube and Kick, send a message now and then so you're counted.

    Does Epic Aura ever reset?

    Every now and then it may be reset so it stays fair for new viewers. You'll always get a heads up in chat before it happens.

    Can I buy or sell Epic Aura?

    No. It has no cash value and can't be bought, sold or swapped for anything outside the stream.

    I linked the wrong account

    Type !unlink on the account you want to remove. Your balance stays with your other accounts and the unlinked one starts from zero.
