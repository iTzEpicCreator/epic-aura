Epic Aura

Our own points currency across Twitch, YouTube and Kick. Earn it by watching, spend it on Apex Legends inspired chat games, giveaways and predictions.

  • Check and flex

    !aura for your balance, !auratop for the richest, !stats and !gametop for game records.

  • Play

    !fight and !ring are our Apex games. Plus !duel, !heist, !gamble, and !claim when a drop lands.

  • Events

    !enter giveaways, !bet on predictions, !contribute to community goals.

  • Full guide

    Odds, limits, linking accounts and more in the Epic Aura guide.

Apex stats

Check Terry's stats for any legend. A banner pops up on stream and the stats get posted in chat.

  • Any legend

    Type the legend's name, like !wraith, !pathfinder or !bangalore, to see Terry's kills, damage and wins with them, plus his banner frame and pose. It shows on stream for about 10 seconds.

  • Current legend

    !main shows the legend Terry is playing right now.

Song requests

Get your tune on stream.

  • On Twitch

    Subs, VIPs and mods type !sr followed by a YouTube link, e.g. !sr https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=.... Everyone else uses the Song Request channel points redeem and pastes the link in the box.

  • On YouTube and Kick

    Everyone can use !sr with a YouTube link.

  • Length limit

    Up to 6 minutes per song.

  • See and manage the queue

    !queue shows what's playing and up next. !remove <number> takes out your own request, using its number from !queue. !src posts this how-to in chat.

  • Head bangers welcome

    Swearing is fine and hardstyle and hardcore are very much encouraged. What's not okay is anything hateful or inappropriate, including tracks that lean heavily on racial slurs.

  • What happens if it's not okay

    Anything we don't like gets removed. If it's clear it was requested to be hateful, that's a warning or a ban.

Channel points

Twitch only. Spend your Epic Coin Points from the channel points menu under the Twitch chat box.

  • Voice changers

    Change Terry's voice for a bit. Dark Angel, Baby, Fart Time and more.

  • Sounds and animations

    Pop a sound effect or animation on screen.

  • Light colours

    Type a colour and Terry's room lights change to match.

  • Make Terry do it

    Hydrate, posture checks, choose his character, change outfit, mask up and more.

  • Not the same as Epic Aura

    Channel points are Twitch only and live in the Twitch menu. Epic Aura works everywhere and uses chat commands.
