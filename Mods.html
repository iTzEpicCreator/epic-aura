Mod guide

While Terry's in a fight, you're the one keeping chat welcoming, fair and fun. Here's what's expected, and how to run the Epic Aura tools well.

The minimum

These are the basics every mod sticks to, every stream, on every platform.

  • Welcome people

    Say hi to new and returning chatters by name, especially first-timers. Answer questions, and point them to !aurahelp when they ask about the games.

  • Keep it positive

    Banter and roasting the gameplay is fine. Picking on a viewer isn't. If a disagreement starts, calm it down or take it to whispers, never argue it out in chat.

  • Be fair and consistent

    Same behaviour, same response, whoever it is. Friends, regulars and VIPs get no special treatment, and nobody gets punished for disagreeing with you.

  • Never use tools for yourself

    No adding Aura to yourself or your friends, no rigging giveaways, no drops timed for your mates. If it would look dodgy on a clip, don't do it.

  • Cover every platform

    Chat comes in from Twitch, YouTube and Kick. Keep an eye on all the ones you mod, so nobody on the smaller platforms gets ignored.

  • Don't backseat

    No telling Terry how to play unless he asks. Remove messages that call out his position for stream snipers, and anything sharing someone's personal info.

  • Represent Epicness

    When Terry's busy, you're the face of the community. Be the kind of chat people want to come back to.

  • Flag it, don't sit on it

    If you're unsure, ask in mod chat or message Terry after stream. Anything serious, tell him straight away, even mid-stream.

Handling problems

Start light and step up. Most people just need a nudge.

  1. Friendly reminder in chat, or a whisper.
  2. Delete the message and give a short timeout (1 to 10 minutes).
  3. Longer timeout if they keep going.
  4. Ban, then let Terry know why after stream.

Ban straight away, no warning, for hate speech, threats, sharing anyone's personal info, sexual content, bots and scam links. Then tell Terry.

During giveaways

Terry runs the giveaway itself. Your job is keeping chat clear, fair and safe while it's on.

  • Explain how to enter

    Lots of people will ask. Reply with !enter to join, or !giveaway to see the prize and ticket price. On paid giveaways, remind them !enter 3 or !enter max buys more tickets.

  • Let the entries flow

    A wall of !enter is expected, so don't time people out for it. Do clear out spam that has nothing to do with the giveaway so the important messages stay readable.

  • Watch for scams

    Fake "you won, DM me" messages and impersonator accounts pop up during giveaways. Ban them on sight and remind chat that winners are only ever announced by BakaOwnage in chat and contacted by Terry himself.

  • Stay neutral

    Never promise anyone a win, hint at who's ahead, or pressure people to spend Aura. The draw is random, and that's the point.

  • Entering yourself

    You can enter like anyone else unless Terry says otherwise. Never ask him for extra entries or a reroll.

  • Handle sore losers

    Congratulate the winner, keep it light with anyone salty, and remind them there'll be more. If someone keeps complaining, it's a normal timeout like anything else.

  • If the winner's gone

    Let Terry decide whether to wait or reroll. If they turn up late, point them to Terry, don't promise anything.

Good moments to jump in

The best mods read the room. A few easy wins:

A raid lands

Welcome the raiders, post !aurainfo, and drop a small !rain so they start with something.

Chat goes quiet

Fire a !drop or start a !ring and get people involved.

New match starts

Open a prediction so chat has something riding on it.

Terry gets a big win

Hype it up, and a !rain to celebrate goes a long way.

Someone's lost everything

Point them to how they earn it back. Don't bail people out with !addaura.

Someone new asks a question

Answer it yourself first, then point them to !aurahelp.

When something breaks

  1. Cancel whatever's stuck so people get refunded: !predict cancel or !ring cancel. If a giveaway is stuck, tell Terry so he can cancel it.
  2. If someone lost Aura to a bug, put it back with !addaura.
  3. Note what happened and roughly when in mod chat, so Terry can check the logs after stream.
